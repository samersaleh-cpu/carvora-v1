International payment requirement

CARVORA Pro is designed to accept customers outside Palestine. The checkout must accept international Visa/Mastercard cards and settle in supported currencies. Subscription activation will only happen after secure provider-side verification.

Payment provider target

Integration pending credentials
Primary candidate: NeoCashIts published Palestinian e-commerce gateway states that it supports recurring payments, card tokenization, and cards from around the world in multiple currencies.
Fallback: Bank of PalestineIts published gateway accepts Visa and Mastercard cards locally and internationally and supports multiple currencies.
What is still required

A merchant account, approved international-card processing, provider API/checkout credentials, and a secure server-side webhook/entitlement service. CARVORA will not expose secret credentials in GitHub Pages and will not unlock Pro from localStorage alone.
